Keep the exact outer eye shapes, dark upper eyelashes, lash corners and lower-eye outline in exactly the same positions. Leave no pupil remnants, dots or colored parts inside either eye. Both eyes must be fully blank white openings ready to receive independent animated iris layers. Do not close the eyes or remove the eyelash outlines. Preserve alpha transparency around the entire character and through the hair gaps, no backdrop, no new elements, no text, no UI, no circle. One character only. This is a technical animation layer, not finished character art. Keep all details outside eye interiors unchanged, same composition and aspect ratio as reference.
